## 🍗 1. The Heritage of St-Hubert Barbecue

Since 1951, [**st-hubert barbecue**](https://st-hubertmenu.ca/) has defined authentic spit-roasted rotisserie chicken across Canada. Slow-roasted in traditional rotisseries, the chicken delivers crisp golden skin, juicy tender meat, and the iconic dipping sauce made from an authentic French-Canadian herbal broth.

Explore the complete sauce guide: [St-Hubert BBQ & Piri-Piri Dipping Sauces](https://st-hubertmenu.ca/menu/sauces/).
